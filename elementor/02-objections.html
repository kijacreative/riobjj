You don’t need to be ready

Three things stop adults from booking a first class. None of them are about Jiu-Jitsu.

01

“I’m not in shape for this.”

Neither was anyone here on day one. Conditioning is what Jiu-Jitsu gives you, not what it asks for.

02

“I’ll get hurt.”

Beginners drill with coaches and upper belts who have been doing this for a decade. Nobody is trying to win against you.

03

“I’ve trained before.”

Then come to an open mat instead. Tell us your belt when you book and we’ll put you in the right room.
