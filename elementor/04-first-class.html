What you are actually signing up for

Your first class, minute by minute

Minutes 0–5

You arrive and someone knows your name

Shoes off at the mat edge. A coach walks you through where to change, hands you a loaner gi and shows you how to tie the belt.

Minutes 5–20

Warm-up, at your pace

Movement drills, not a fitness test. Stop when you need to. Water is by the door and nobody is counting.

Minutes 20–45

Two techniques, slowly, with a partner

One escape and one control position. You will practise both with an upper belt who has taught them a hundred times.

Minutes 45–60

Light practice, then a handshake

Positional rounds at whatever intensity you choose. Beginners are never thrown into open rolling on day one.
